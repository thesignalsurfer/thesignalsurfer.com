How to read the percentages: they're the share of exam questions per domain. RF is only 15%, but it underpins everything else — that's why it's Module 1. The three 20% domains (Regulations, Protocols, Validation) are where the most marks live, so they earn the most study time.
01

RF Fundamentals

15%

Waves, frequency & wavelength, amplitude & phase, the 2.4/5/6 GHz bands, RF behaviour (reflection, absorption, scattering, refraction, diffraction), multipath & Fresnel zone, the decibel (3s & 10s), dBm/dBi/dBd, RSSI, noise floor, SNR, and EIRP.

Regulations & Standards

20%

Regulatory bodies (FCC, ETSI, Ofcom, ISED), the IEEE vs Wi-Fi Alliance, channels & channel widths, the 802.11 PHY amendments (a/b/g/n/ac/ax), spread spectrum, modulation & coding (BPSK, QPSK, QAM, OFDM, OFDMA), and power/regulatory limits.

Suggested run to exam day

Weeks 1–2: Module 1 (RF) → Module 2 (Standards). Week 3: Module 3 (Protocols). Week 4: Modules 4 & 5 (Design + Security). Week 5: Module 6 (Validation), then revisit weak spots. Week 6: full practice exams, timed, reviewing every wrong answer. Keep the official objectives PDF open as your checklist throughout.
